Evolution of Money

A concept onboarding for a payments app that tells 3,000 years of money in six steps. 36,000 particles reshape from barter goods into a cowrie shell, a Lydian coin, a Jiaozi note, a payment card and Bitcoin. The morph is tied to your finger, so you can pause or reverse it mid-swipe.
